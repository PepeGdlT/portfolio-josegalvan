Featured case study · Machine Learning

Predicting ATP match outcomes from historical, pre-match information.

A reproducible machine-learning workflow that builds player state chronologically, compares model families with walk-forward validation, and evaluates the selected ensemble on matches that occur strictly after training.

01 · Problem

A prediction must only know what was available before the match.

ATP outcomes depend on player strength, surface, form, fatigue and matchup history. The difficult engineering constraint is temporal: rankings, ratings and form must be reconstructed in order, without future information leaking into training features.

02 · Data & features

Historical match records become evolving player state.

The pipeline processes professional ATP matches chronologically. Each row is transformed into comparative pre-match features, then the players’ state is updated only after that match has been observed.

01

Ratings

Global, surface and recent ELO signals.

02

Ranking

Rank and ranking-point differences, with missing-value awareness.

03

Form

Recent win rates, quality-adjusted form and streaks.

04

Matchup

Head-to-head history and surface context.

05

Activity

Rest, recent matches and minutes played.

06

Serve & return

Historical rates and the amount of evidence behind them.

03 · Approach

Compare diverse models, then combine their out-of-sample signals.

Six model families were tested through 16 configurations: logistic regression with PCA, Random Forest, Extra Trees, Histogram Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, and XGBoost with PCA. The final model is a logistic stack trained on out-of-fold probabilities from those families plus global and surface ELO priors.

  1. 01

    Chronological features

    Build every predictor from past matches only.

  2. 02

    Walk-forward search

    Validate candidates on 2023, 2024 and 2025 folds.

  3. 03

    Probability-first selection

    Use log loss as the primary metric, with AUC, Brier and calibration checks.

  4. 04

    Strict future test

    Freeze selection before evaluating the 484-match external holdout.
